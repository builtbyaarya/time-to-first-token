# Data licence

Two kinds of data come out of this project, and they are licensed differently.

## 1. The data pack snapshot in this repository: CC BY 4.0

`data/pack.json`, the research files in `data/research/` and `docs/sources.md` are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/. You may share and adapt them for any purpose, including commercially, as long as you credit "Time to First Token data pack, Aarya, builtbyaarya" and say if you changed anything.

A snapshot is frozen at the version date it carries (`version` and `accessed` in the file) and is never refreshed in place. A new snapshot is cut from the weekly pack each quarter and committed here under the same licence.

Each value in the pack is itself a reading from a published source named in the value. Those sources keep their own rights; the pack records the figure, the publisher, the URL, the date and at most a few words of quotation, never a copied table.

## 2. The weekly refreshed pack and the sold items: commercial licence

The weekly refreshed data pack, custom cited studies, the hosted team workspace and the enterprise data-pack licence are sold under a separate commercial licence and are not released under CC BY 4.0 until they become the next quarterly snapshot. Terms are agreed per customer; to ask, open a GitHub issue titled "Commercial use".

## The code: MIT

Everything that is not data (`index.html`, `css/`, `js/`, `scripts/`, `docs/methodology.md`) is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.

Nothing in this project is engineering, legal or investment advice, and no study it produces is a quote.
