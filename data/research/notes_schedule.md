# Notes: permitting, construction schedule and commissioning pack (accessed 2026-10-24)

## Looked for and found
- EPA thresholds: Title V 100 tpy, HAP 10/25 tpy (EPA Title V page); PSD 250 tpy unlisted / 100 tpy listed and 18-month construction-start lapse (40 CFR 52.21 on eCFR); EPA data center Clean Air Act hub (Sept 2025, updated Oct 2026) gives 500 h/yr PTE default for emergency generators and 50 h non-emergency allowance.
- State air permit timelines: Virginia DEQ (synthetic minor 4-6 mo, state major + Title V 6-12 mo, PSD + Title V 1-2 yr; Tier 4 rule from July 1, 2026) and Pennsylvania DEP (minor 6-9 mo, major 9-15 mo) from ALL4 webinar decks; TCEQ target timeframes (PBR 45 d, standard permit w/ notice 150 d, NSR 240 d, federal NSR/PSD 330 d); Maricopa County rules (Non-Title V 90 d to 9 mo max; Title V 18 mo max, proposed permit 9 mo).
- NEPA: CEQ Jan 2025 report (EIS mean 3.8 yr, median 2.5 yr for 2021-24; EA average 9.6 months DOT 2021-23); CE 7 months from USFS study via Roosevelt Institute.
- Zoning/site plan/building permit: Loudoun eliminated by-right (March 2025); Prince William County FY26 metrics (site plan 129 county + 269 applicant days, ~13 months; building first review 3.54 wk; major new construction 152 county days); Mesa AZ 18 working days first review (5 super-expedited).
- Water: LBNL 2024 report (176 TWh 2023, 325-580 TWh 2028, PUE 1.4 to 1.15-1.35, WUE 0.36 to 0.45-0.48 L/kWh); 64 billion liters direct water 2023 only via secondary (Civil Beat/The Conversation). NPDES: WA Ecology individual permit 210 + 36 days; Ohio draft general NPDES permit for data centers (Dec 2025).
- Construction durations: JLARC VA 12-18 months per building; ECMag 18-24 months shell; Archdesk hyperscale 18-24 months, 3-6 year full cycle; Opendock stage windows (shell 1-6, power 4-10, cooling 6-12, IT 10-18, Cx 18-24); Mastt phase ranges (planning 3-6, permits 6-18, design 6-12, construction 12-24, commissioning 3-6 months).
- Benchmarks: xAI Colossus 122 days (retrofit); Crusoe Abilene June 2024 start, 2 buildings/200 MW+ live by Oct 2025, phase 2 March 2025 to mid-2026; Applied Digital Polaris Forge 1 Building 1 phased 50 MW RFS Oct and Nov 2025; Microsoft Fairwater online target early 2026; Meta Hyperion 2025-2030.
- Commissioning: L1-L5 definitions (Archdesk, Peter Wicks); 10-14 weeks for L1-L5 on $60M+ project; IST 4-6 weeks air-cooled vs 10-14 weeks liquid-cooled AI hall; Uptime TCCF site visit 3-5 days ~1 month after construction ends, after L5.
- Tiers: Uptime definitions (I-IV) from uptimeinstitute.com/tiers; Tier IV vs III cost only from a 2012 Uptime survey ($22k vs $20k per kW, ~10%).
- Delays: JLL 57% of projects delayed 3+ months in 2025; Uptime 2025 survey (42% enterprise, 52% colo reported capital project delays; driver shares); 30-50% of 16 GW US pipeline slipping (secondary); PW Digital Gateway rezoning voided 20 months after approval; Wood Mackenzie lead times (GSU 143 wk, LPT 128 wk, switchgear 44 wk); JLL 42-week average equipment lead time; LBNL Queued Up median ~5 years; Dominion 4-7 years for >100 MW; ERCOT Batch Zero 9 months; EMT study 6-9 months.

## Could not find / caveats
- EPA does not publish a target processing time for PSD or minor NSR permits; only state targets (TCEQ, Maricopa) and consultant estimates (VA, PA) were available. No published figures for Georgia EPD, Ohio EPA or Arizona ADEQ air permit durations.
- LBNL water totals in billion liters could not be read from the PDF text (the water section was not in the fetched text); the 64 billion liter figure is from a secondary source quoting LBNL.
- No source gives separate durations for geotechnical survey, grading/civil, foundations alone, steel alone, electrical alone or mechanical alone; only overlapping stage windows (Opendock) and phase ranges (Mastt). No per-level durations for L1-L4 individually.
- Turner & Townsend Data Centre Cost Index 2025 pages returned client errors; no T&T delay statistics captured.
- No source quantifying schedule difference between Tier III and Tier IV; cost premium only from a 2012 survey.
- No typical slip-in-months statistic beyond "3+ months" (JLL); no transformer-specific delay statistic beyond lead times.
- Loudoun County publishes no special exception duration; Prince William site plan metrics used as the Virginia proxy. No Maricopa County zoning timeline found; Mesa building review used for AZ.
- Several construction-duration sources are vendor/consultant blogs (Opendock, Mastt, Archdesk), flagged in notes; JLARC and company press releases are the primary ones.
