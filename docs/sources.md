## Assumptions

- `gas_supply_months`: 18 months. No public figure found for a data-center-scale lateral and firm supply agreement; 18 months (12 to 30) is our assumption. It gates the onsite plant's connection. No public figure for a lateral plus a firm supply agreement at this scale; the range is wide on purpose.
- `lead_accelerator_months`: 9 months. No public allocation lead time for NVL72-class racks; 9 months (6 to 12) is our assumption, released from the basis of design. Supply Chain Management Review reports flagship GPU systems at about 8 to 16 weeks in some channels in 2026; rack-scale allocations run longer, so 9 months stays our assumption.
- `transmission_build_months`: 0 months. Utility line works are counted inside the energization wait above rather than as a separate task; a dedicated 345 kV line would add years (Grid Strategies: 6 to 10 years for large builds). Line works sit inside the utility energization wait, which already comes from utility and JLL figures; a separate line would double count.
- `grading_civil_months`: 3 months. No public duration found for the civil package alone; 3 months (2 to 4) is our assumption, inside the industry guide's construction phase. The Mastt guide's construction phase contains the civil package but does not split it out.
- `air_cooling_fitout_factor`: 1.0 ratio. No evidence that air-cooled fit-out is faster or slower; factor 1. Lead times now differ by equipment (Terrapin Construction Group), and the plan carries those; no source says the installation itself is faster or slower.
- `cluster_bringup_months`: 1 months. No public figure; one month is our assumption for burn-in after the racks are powered. Context: xAI's Colossus went from start to first training run in 122 days, which includes bring-up; no separate bring-up figure is published.
- `redundancy_2n_electrical_time_factor`: 1.1 ratio. A second full power path adds equipment and testing; 10% more fit-out time is our assumption.
- `construction_scale_exponent`: 0.25 exponent. Construction durations scale as (MW/100)^0.25, so 1 GW takes about 1.8x as long as 100 MW per task with the work phased across buildings. Our assumption, calibrated against the JLARC 12 to 18 month building and the 5-year Meta Hyperion campus. Checked against the 12 to 18 month building in Virginia's JLARC study (the state legislature's review commission) and Meta's 5-year Hyperion campus.
- `stress_gpu_months`: 3 months. No public allocation statistic; one quarter is our assumption. One quarter, the usual unit in which GPU allocations are quoted; no public statistic on slips.
- `risk_transformer_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_interconnection_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_permit_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_turbine_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_utility_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_gpu_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `risk_labour_mitigation`: 0 . Mitigation wording is ours; the figures it cites are in the pack.
- `solar_bess_pv_ratio`: 3 ratio. Three kW of PV per kW served, our assumption for a daytime-plus-storage plant. Sizing rule for a plant that serves the load around the clock from daytime sun and storage; it sets cost, not schedule.
- `compute_capex_per_mw__rubin`: 32.3 USD million per MW IT. No public Rubin pricing; the GB300 figure per MW is carried over as an assumption.
